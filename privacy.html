Engineering Renaissance

Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This page explains what the Engineering Renaissance member portal at engineeringrenaissance.org/portal stores and how it's used. Engineering Renaissance is a student engineering club at Clovis Community College. The public pages of this website don't collect anything about visitors.

What the portal stores

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo. The portal never sees your Google password.
  • What you enter: the join form (name, pronouns if you give them, major, subteams and a note), anything you add to your profile, and the pages, tasks, announcements, events and comments you write.
  • Lab hours: when you clock in and out, the activity you pick, and any fix requests and approvals.
  • Location check, only if the club turns it on: whether you were at the lab when you clocked in. Your actual location is never saved.
  • Lead and admin actions: approvals, edits to hours, role changes and similar actions are recorded in an audit log.
  • Announcements from Discord: when an officer mentions the portal bot in the club’s Discord server, the post’s text, the poster’s Discord display name and profile picture, and a link to the post are copied into the portal’s announcements.
  • Discord username, only if you add it to your profile: the portal bot uses it to find you in the club’s Discord server and send you a direct message when someone assigns you a task, or when you’ve been clocked in longer than the club’s limit. Remove it from your profile and the messages stop.

Google Drive

If you attach Drive files to the portal, it asks for Google's narrowest Drive permission. It can open only the files you pick and the spreadsheets you export from it, and nothing else in your Drive. Files keep their own Drive sharing settings.

Who can see it

Only members an admin has approved can see portal content. Club leads and admins can see members' hours and profiles to run the club and report hours. We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Where it's kept

Portal data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the United States. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

How long it's kept

Your information stays while you're a member. When you leave, an admin marks you inactive so your logged hours stay in the club's records. To have your account and information deleted, email us.
